FitBeat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

The short version. FitBeat has no accounts and no servers. Your music, your photos, your library and your settings stay on your phone. We cannot see them, and we do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or usage data of any kind.

Who we are

FitBeat is developed and published by AILINK, based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Questions about this policy can be sent to liwei.rong@ailink.ca.

What FitBeat stores, and where

Everything below is stored in FitBeat's private storage area on your device. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Audio files you add. When you add a song, FitBeat copies the file into its own storage so playback keeps working if you move or delete the original.
  • Cover photos you choose. A copy of the image you select is stored the same way.
  • Track details. Title, artist, tempo and any notes you type, kept in a small file on the device.
  • Your settings. Default tempo, time signature and beat sound.

Removing a track deletes its copied audio and cover. Uninstalling FitBeat deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side, because there is no our side.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Files and media. Only to read the specific audio file you pick. FitBeat does not scan or index your storage.
  • Photos. Only to read the specific image you choose as a cover. On Android 13 and later this uses the system photo picker, which gives FitBeat access to that one image and nothing else.
  • Notifications and foreground service (Android). Used to keep playback running while your screen is off or you switch apps, and to show the playback notification. Nothing is sent through them.

Purchases

FitBeat offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional training sessions. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your name, email address, or any payment information — the store tells the app only whether the purchase is active.

Apple and Google process this transaction under their own privacy policies: Apple and Google.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry SDKs.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health-sensor access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — we hold none to sell.

Children

FitBeat is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, none is knowingly collected from children.

Your rights

Under Quebec's Law 25, Canada's PIPEDA, the EU GDPR and similar laws, you have rights of access, correction and deletion over personal information a company holds about you. FitBeat holds none. All the data the app creates lives on your device and is under your direct control: delete a track to remove its files, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

If you have made a purchase and want it reviewed, that request goes to Apple or Google, who hold the transaction record.

Health and safety

FitBeat's training sessions set a step cadence. They are not medical or coaching advice, and they are not a substitute for either. Consult a qualified professional before beginning hard training, and stop if something hurts.

Changes to this policy

If FitBeat ever begins collecting data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
